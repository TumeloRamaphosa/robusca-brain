## Research (the finding that changed everything)

Ran the sweep: `research/last30days/2026-08-22-viral-and-agent-narrative.md`, 31 sourced facts.

**The AI agent category is in a credibility recession and we are accidentally built for it.**
Gartner's first dedicated Agentic AI Hype Cycle (April 2026) named `agent-washing` as an
explicit procurement hazard and estimates only ~130 of thousands of vendors are genuinely
agentic. MIT found ~95% of enterprise GenAI pilots delivered no measurable P&L impact.
Gartner projects >40% of agentic projects cancelled by end-2027.

The buyer's checklist for a *real* agent is: persistent memory, decision trace, permissions,
governance, isolation, outcome-based pricing. That is a description of Business Ghosts +
Agent OS + NestVM + Execution Exchange. We have the answers to the exam everyone just
started grading. We have been describing them as features instead of as answers.

Format mechanics also moved hard: hook window is now 1–3s (not 5s), hot-take/investigator
hooks beat story hooks ~20x, greenscreen beats talking head ~2.6x, 90s+ beats sub-15s ~31x,
and 3-second retention is the metric that matters, not views.

## Calls Made

1. **Positioning spine: Memory → Workforce → Market.** Collapses seven brands and five layers
   into three nouns that each require the previous one. Doubles as roadmap and pricing ladder.
   The structural story is an escalator — talent up, work down, one infrastructure.
2. **Wedge: "Most AI agents are fake. Here's the test. Here's us taking it."** Contrarian
   enough for reach, evidenced enough for trust, and it sets the exam on ground we win.
3. **Launch Business Ghost alone** at R950/company/month unlimited seats — not the ecosystem.
   A launch is concentration; eight simultaneous launches is zero. Ghost demos in 30 seconds,
   needs no marketplace liquidity, and makes Super Agents the obvious next sale.
4. **Execution Exchange deferred to phase 4.** Two-sided cold start; launching it into an empty
   room burns its only first impression. Ghost solves its supply side for free.
5. **Midnight Founders Club is the content engine, not a product.** Weekly ritual = self-supplying
   content + community + talent pipeline. Don't charge for it early.
6. **Rename Agentic Rise → Studex Rise.** "Agentic" is the tell-word we'd be campaigning
   against. Free to fix now, a news cycle later.
7. **Token rail out of all launch material.** See blockers.

## Blockers I Flagged to Tumelo

- **No receipts.** Zero instrumented outcome numbers anywhere for NtechLab, Pharmasyntez, ART
  Engineering MDC or Project Phoenix. A campaign premised on "everyone else overclaims" cannot
  itself overclaim. This gates Phase 1.
- **Token rail is a licensed financial services business.** In SA that flow plausibly needs FSCA
  CASP licensing + FIC registration + a *separate* FinSurv authorisation for the cross-border
  leg. Utility tokens are explicitly not carved out. Needs an attorney. Independently of the
  law: showing enterprise procurement an unlicensed token while pitching governance is
  self-refuting and selects for crypto speculators over the R20k/month buyer.
- **API keys from the June incident still not confirmed rotated.** A launch points scrutiny at
  our infrastructure. Doing that with known-exposed credentials while selling auditability is
  the worst combination available. Blocks Phase 1.
- **"14 years of AI"** is unsubstantiated in our own records and will be challenged.
